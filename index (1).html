Questions, answered

Dallas roofing FAQs

How much does a new roof cost in Dallas, TX?

Most asphalt shingle replacements land between $9,500 and $22,000 for a 2,000–3,000 sq ft home — roughly $4.50–$7.00 per square foot installed. Metal runs $18,000–$45,000 and tile or slate higher. We quote a fixed price after inspection, so what you sign is what you pay.

Will my insurance cover hail damage to my roof in Texas?

Usually yes. Texas homeowners policies cover sudden storm damage from hail and wind. We document damage, provide the itemized scope (Xactimate), and meet your adjuster on the property. You typically pay only your deductible plus any upgrades you choose.

How long does a roof replacement take?

One to two days for most single-family Dallas homes. Steeper, larger or multi-plane roofs may need three to five days. You get a written schedule up front, and we clean the yard with a magnet sweep every evening.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes — every inspection is free and includes a 21-point checklist and a photo report. There is no obligation and no charge for insurance documentation visits.

Do you handle the City of Dallas permit?

Always. We pull the permit, post it on site, schedule the municipal inspection and deliver the sign-off with your closing documents. Unpermitted work can void warranties and complicate a home sale.

How fast can you stop an active leak?

Our emergency line answers 24/7, and we target same-day or next-day emergency tarping in the Dallas area. Permanent repairs typically follow in 24–48 hours, once the cause is documented for your claim.
